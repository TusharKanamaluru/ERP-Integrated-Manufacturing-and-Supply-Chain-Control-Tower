# Procurement Analytics

## Objective

The objective of this module is to analyze procurement spending patterns, supplier dependence, and sourcing efficiency within a high-precision machining environment.

The analysis uses supplier performance data, material purchasing requirements, and annual procurement spend metrics.

---

## Procurement Overview

The organization procures:

- Raw Materials
- Bought-Out Components
- Cutting Tools
- Metrology Equipment
- Industrial Consumables

These purchases support day-to-day manufacturing operations and production continuity.

## Key Findings

### Supplier Concentration

A significant portion of procurement spend is concentrated among a small number of suppliers, particularly Tata Steel and SKF.

### Imported Material Dependency

Several critical production inputs such as bearings, linear guideways, tooling, and metrology equipment rely on imported suppliers, increasing lead-time exposure.

### Lead-Time Variability

Longer lead times for Japanese and European suppliers require careful inventory planning and safety stock management.

### Strategic Suppliers

Steel suppliers, tooling suppliers, and bearing manufacturers were identified as critical suppliers due to their direct impact on production continuity.

---

## Recommendations

- Develop alternate sources for critical imported components.
- Track supplier lead-time performance through monthly reviews.
- Reduce procurement risk through supplier diversification.
- Align procurement planning with inventory optimization policies.

---

## Conclusion

Procurement analytics improves visibility into purchasing activities and supplier dependency, supporting more informed sourcing and inventory management decisions.
