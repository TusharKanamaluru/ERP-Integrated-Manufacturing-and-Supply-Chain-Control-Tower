# Supplier Performance Analytics

## Objective

The objective of this module is to evaluate supplier performance using delivery, quality, lead-time, and procurement-spend metrics to support sourcing and supply chain decision-making.

The analysis is based on the supplier master dataset located in the `data` folder.

## Key Findings

### Supplier Reliability

Mitutoyo and Sandvik Coromant demonstrate the strongest overall performance based on delivery reliability and quality metrics.

### Supply Risk

Imported components sourced from Sweden and Japan exhibit longer lead times, increasing planning complexity and inventory requirements.

### Spend Distribution

Procurement expenditure is concentrated among a small number of strategic suppliers, particularly Tata Steel and SKF.

### Critical Suppliers

Steel suppliers, tooling providers, and bearing vendors directly influence machining capacity and production continuity.

---

## Recommendations

- Monitor lead-time trends for imported components.
- Maintain supplier scorecards for periodic performance reviews.
- Develop secondary sourcing options for critical components.
- Integrate supplier-performance metrics into procurement planning activities.

---

## Conclusion

Supplier-performance monitoring enables better sourcing decisions and supports more resilient supply chain operations by improving visibility into delivery, quality, and procurement metrics.
